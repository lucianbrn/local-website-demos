Our Restaurant

A taste of home, in Fairfax

Located in Fairfax, Virginia, Baku Delicious brings you the authentic flavours of Azerbaijani, Turkish, Georgian, Slavic and Uzbek cuisine — every dish crafted to remind you of home, in a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

✦

The Bakery

Fresh pastries and cakes from Azerbaijani tradition — pakhlava layered with walnut and honey, shekerbura, kete, honey cake, Napoleon cake and Spartak cake.

✦

The Café

A cosy café serving breakfast every day, Azerbaijani tea and Turkish coffee, and mains from across the Caucasus and Central Asia.

✦

The Shop

An Azerbaijani shop alongside the café — stop in for the tastes and ingredients of home, from sweets and teas to regional specialities.
