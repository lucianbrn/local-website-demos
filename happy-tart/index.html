From Our Kitchen

Baked Without Gluten. Ever.

The Happy Tart has been Northern Virginia’s dedicated gluten-and-wheat-free bakery since 2012, and under new ownership since 2025, we’re bringing fresh energy to the kitchen.

Expect bold, seasonal flavors, classic French technique, and real butter, chocolate, and fruit in everything we bake — all from our 100% gluten-free and wheat-free kitchen.

Our Promise

A Kitchen Where Gluten Never Enters

For more than a decade, we’ve scratch-baked French-style pies, breads and desserts in a kitchen where gluten never enters — so our pie crust is flaky, golden, and just as good as the one you remember.

That means guests with celiac disease, gluten intolerance, or dairy allergies can all be delighted at the table.
