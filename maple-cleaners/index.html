Services

Everything below is a service Maple Cleaners lists on its own site. For current pricing, please call — prices vary by garment and care instructions.

👔

Dry cleaning

Suits, slacks, dresses, jackets, blazers, overcoats, skirts and blouses — including designer pieces that need hand pressing and special care.

👕

Shirts & laundry

Men’s cotton shirts laundered and pressed, plus everyday laundry finished and ready to wear.

🧺

Wash, dry & fold

A drop-off laundry service for when there is no time in the day — drop it off and pick it up washed, dried and folded.

✂️

Alterations & tailoring

An on-site tailor for hemming, zipper fixes and professional dress alterations — plus patch and rank sewing for uniforms.

🛏️

Household items

Comforters, blankets, linens, pillows, small rugs, tablecloths and draperies — the bulky items that do not fit a home machine.

👗

Special & formal pieces

Wedding gowns (including cleaning and preservation), gowns and long dresses, choir robes, Indian saris and blouses, leather, tuxedos and uniforms.

🎖️

Military uniforms

Cleaning and tailoring for Army, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard, Marine Corps and Space Force uniforms — creases pressed or made new, patches and ranks sewn on.

🧴

Spot & stain removal

Tell the counter about ink, wine, coffee, dressing, makeup or pet stains when you drop off — stained items are treated separately for the best result.

⚡

Same-day & urgent service

Same-day dry cleaning Monday to Saturday for trips, weddings and flights — and a 1-hour service for ultra emergencies. Call ahead so staff can confirm timing for your garment.

Bring your metal wire hangers back with you — Maple Cleaners recycles them, since they cannot go in single-stream recycling bins.

How it works

Drop off in the Jades Shopping Center, or schedule pickup and delivery by phone.

1

Drop off or call for pickup

Bring garments to 348 Maple Ave W in Vienna — free parking in the Jades Shopping Center, next to Social Burger and Organic Nails & Spa. Pickup and delivery is offered across Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C. and Maryland; call to schedule.

2

Tell us what needs care

Counter staff tag each garment with its own label and give you an invoice receipt. Point out any stains or special care instructions so they can be treated separately.

3

Clean, press, ready

Garments are cleaned, pressed and assembled in their on-site plant. Same-day orders dropped off in the morning can be ready the same day — call if you are up against a deadline.
