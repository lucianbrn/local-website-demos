How it works

Drop off in the Jades Shopping Center, or schedule pickup and delivery by phone.

1

Drop off or call for pickup

Bring garments to 348 Maple Ave W in Vienna — free parking in the Jades Shopping Center, next to Social Burger and Organic Nails & Spa. Pickup and delivery is offered across Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C. and Maryland; call to schedule.

2

Tell us what needs care

Counter staff tag each garment with its own label and give you an invoice receipt. Point out any stains or special care instructions so they can be treated separately.

3

Clean, press, ready

Garments are cleaned, pressed and assembled in their on-site plant. Same-day orders dropped off in the morning can be ready the same day — call if you are up against a deadline.
