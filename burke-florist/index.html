For every occasion

Occasions & Services

From a simple bouquet to a full wedding, the shop handles the flowers — and the delivery or pickup.

Everyday & Special Occasions

Floral arrangements and centerpieces for everyday, special occasions, holidays, and parties — plus fruit and gourmet baskets and silk arrangements.

  • Designer's Choice: not sure what you'd like? Let the shop decide — they'll create a beautiful vase arrangement.
  • Plants, too: peace lilies, dish gardens, succulent gardens, and phalaenopsis orchids.
  • Add something special: local honey, a coconut soy-wax candle, chocolate-covered pretzels, or a plush bear, dog, or cat.

Weddings

Bouquets, ceremony settings, and reception decor — including flower arches and floral art.

  • Planning a wedding? Email burkeflorist@hotmail.com to set up a bridal consultation.
  • Beyond weddings — custom designs for corporate lunches, parties, and events of every kind.

Sympathy & Funerals

Tasteful funeral arrangements, prepared with care:

  • Casket sprays
  • Standing sprays
  • Wreaths, hearts & crosses
  • Baskets & plants

Church Altars

Church altar arrangements, made to suit the service and the space.

Prom & Homecoming

Corsages and boutonnieres for prom and homecoming — order ahead by phone so yours is ready for the night.

Delivery, Pickup & Wire Orders

The shop is open Monday–Saturday for walk-in customers, local deliveries, wire orders, and special occasions — and you can call ahead to arrange a curbside pickup. Deliveries generally run 10 AM–3 PM, and 9 AM–2 PM on Saturdays. If a flower isn't in the shop, they'll substitute something of equal or greater value, so your delivery still arrives on time.

Shopping by style? Their own collections include Rustic, The Classic Style, Vintage, Country Style, Monochromatic, Wildflowers, Ray of Sunshine, and Nothin But Color.
