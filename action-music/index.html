Our Story

Since 1997

Action Music, LTD was established in 1997 to provide the kind of fretted instrument store that owner Matt Baker felt was missing in the Washington, D.C. and Northern Virginia area at the time: a local, independent, repair-oriented shop. After working as a touring tech, Matt wanted a store that stocked the gear you expect — and everything around it, too.

We are grateful for the local community's continued support, which has enabled Action Music to thrive. We strive to be upfront and transparent in all our transactions, because clear communication is essential to good service and reputation.
