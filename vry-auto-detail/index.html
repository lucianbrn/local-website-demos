Detailing Services

Three levels of detail. One way to price them: ask.

These are the three detail services exactly as VRY describes them on its own site — what's included and how long each takes. The price is set when they build your plan.

Signature

The VRY Detail

About 4–5 hours

Their signature and most thorough detailing service — it leaves your car looking brand new. Everything in the Regular Detail, plus:

  • Motor wash
  • 3-step buffing to remove scratches
  • Trim restoration
  • Paint, leather & fabric protection
No set price — quoted as a personalized plan.

Regular Detail

About 3–4 hours

The complete detail of the exterior and interior — improves the overall look and feel of the car, like when you first got it:

  • High-quality wax + 1-step buffing
  • Deep interior cleaning — shampoo & vacuum
  • Leather & vinyl dressings
  • Carpets, floor mats & upholstery seats
No set price — quoted as a personalized plan.

Beyond the Detail Shop

Fleets, dealerships — and buildings, too

VRY is more than a detail shop. The same company runs mobile programs for businesses and a commercial cleaning division.

Fleet Service

Mobile washing and detailing for commercial fleets: hand wash, wheels and tires, interiors shampooed and deep-cleaned. A regular program, VRY notes, contributes to extending the lifetime of the vehicles.

Dealership Services

VRY can manage a dealership's entire detailing operation — new-vehicle prep, pre-owned reconditioning, "white glove" customer delivery and weekly lot wash programs — with a mobile unit that comes to your location by appointment.

Commercial Cleaning

As an ISSA member, VRY cleans and disinfects workplaces: office and building cleaning, sanitizing and disinfection services, deep cleaning, and floor deep cleaning.
