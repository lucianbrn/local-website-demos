Servicing & repairs

A family of clock experts, for the life of your clock

We will help you with both repairs and servicing when you need them — it's part of clock ownership, and it's part of what this shop has done since 1975.

Every 3 years

Clock servicing

Mechanical clocks should be serviced with new oil every 3 years. Servicing your clock will allow it to run smoothly for many more years to come. We provide this service — and can possibly teach you to do the same.

In-house

Clock repairs

If your clock is beyond servicing, we repair fine mechanical clocks in-house. Bring it to the shop and talk it through with a family of clock experts.

Bringing a clock in? Remember to remove any pendulum or weights and bring them in separately.
