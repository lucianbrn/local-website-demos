Our story

From Beirut to Broad Street

The short version of a long family trade — told on the shop's own site, and worth telling right.

1977

Bedros Doudaklian emigrates with his Armenian family from Beirut, Lebanon, and founds Bedo's Leatherworks on a shoemaker's knowledge and long hours.

1991

Mr. Bedo passes away, leaving the legacy he built to his youngest son Steve — trained at his side in the ins and outs of the trade.

1998

Growth moves the shop to 412 W. Broad Street in Falls Church — its home ever since.

45+ yrs

Serving Falls Church and customers worldwide, with craftsmanship recognized through several awards — and over 300 repair videos on the shop's YouTube channel.

Mail-in repair

Not local? Ship it to the bench.

Plenty of Bedo's work arrives by mail. Here's the shop's own process, exactly as they run it — full instructions and the shipping form live on their official shipping page.

1

Email photos first

Send pictures and a description of what you'd like done to bedos@yahoo.com for a rough estimate.

2

Ship it with the form

Print the shop's shipping form, fill it in, and include it in the box with your item.

3

It gets inspected

Once it arrives, Steve inspects the piece and works out a plan — and the real cost.

4

You talk it over

He contacts you by phone or FaceTime to discuss the details. Agreed? The invoice is paid by Visa or Mastercard and the work is scheduled.

5

Finished & shipped back

When it's done, you get pictures — and tracking information once it's boxed up and on its way.
