One stove. A few family recipes. That's it.

In 2021 we set up a small kitchen in the back of a Vienna gas station and started cooking the Punjabi food we grew up on. No dining room. No big sign. Just really good food.

One person tried it. They told a friend. Now people drive in from all over Northern Virginia for a plate of chaat. That is how we got our name — Chit Chaat means a little chit-chat over a plate of chaat, and it still happens here every day.

Typical Punjabi dishes make up most of the menu — home-style cooking, fresh spices, and a depth of flavour that only comes from slow cooking. The samosa burrito, dreamed up by Maggie Sukhwani, one of the Puri daughters, is the spin on a snack favourite that people keep coming back for.
