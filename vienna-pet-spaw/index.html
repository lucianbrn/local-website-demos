New Grooming Clients

Join the Waitlist — Two Steps

We are always accepting new clients for Spaw Bath and nail trim appointments — please email to book. New to our grooming salon? We currently have a waitlist for prospective grooming clients:

  1. Fill out our New Client Form (Google Form, linked from the “New Clients & Contact” page at viennapetspaw.com). Don’t be afraid to over share — the more we know about your pet, the better we can serve you both. Completion of the form, including submission of your most recent vaccination record, is required prior to your first visit.
  2. AND send an email to appointments@viennapetspaw.com stating that you would like to be added to our waiting list. If you do not do both, we will not be able to add you to the list.

We will email you back confirming that you have been added, and let you know what our current wait is. If you have trouble with the Google form, let us know and we will send it to you in a different format.

Good to Know

Before Your Visit

  • Vaccinations RequiredAll clients, without exception, must be vaccinated for Rabies and Canine Distemper. We strongly advise Bordetella and Canine Influenza vaccination as well.
  • TimingWe request a 3–4 hour window for all grooming appointments (excluding Special Handling or Express Service). There is a 15-minute late grace period.
  • First Visit FormsAll clients fill out a Client Release Form at their pet’s first appointment. A High Risk Groom Release Form is required for senior dogs (10+), moderate to severe matting, or other health concerns.
  • Payment — CashlessWe accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express and Discover, plus Apple Pay & Google Pay. Cash tips are always appreciated.
  • Parking & Drop-OffWe have a 5-minute pet drop-off spot in front of our store for drop-offs and pick-ups only. There is also a parking lot directly behind the building, street parking, and garage and municipal lots nearby.
  • CatsWe do not offer cat grooming. We can clip their nails for $15 — please call ahead to ensure a feline-friendly staff member is available.
  • Dogs Other Salons Turned AwayIf your dog has been banned from another grooming salon, you can still bring him or her in — we will patiently work with your pup. Special Handling fees may apply.
  • Pick-UpPlease pick your dog up within four hours for grooming and two hours for bathing — we do not have the space to provide doggy daycare services.
