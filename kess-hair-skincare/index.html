Our Story

Kess — previously “Salon Rovina” — opened in 2008 in Arlington. The salon has lots of clean, open spaces and plenty of natural light, with a hint of cultural identity in hand-carved wooden artwork created by a local Sri Lankan artist. A family team, decades of combined experience, and one simple idea: everyone is beautiful — we just make it natural and obvious.
