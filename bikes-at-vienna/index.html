What we ride

The bikes other shops don't carry

The shop provides support to a segment of the cycling community that is largely underserved in the mainstream bike world. These are its specialty wheeled products:

Recumbent Bikes

Ride in a relaxed position that's easy on joints — greater comfort and ease on longer rides with less fatigue, in a more aerodynamic position that lets you go faster than a conventional bike. The shop carries a variety of recumbent bikes.

Try one by appointment

Recumbent Trikes

Three wheels, same laid-back recumbent comfort — no balancing act, just sit back and pedal. The shop carries a variety of recumbent trikes alongside its two-wheelers.

Try one by appointment

Folding Bikes

Anywhere you have a bit of space, you can tuck a folding bike. Take one on public transportation and explore urban spaces, carry it in your RV for trails and greenways — or take it on your next trip overseas.

Try one by appointment

Adaptive Bikes

One of the shop's great pleasures is helping riders with a wide range of abilities enjoy cycling — adults and young people alike. From modifying standard bikes and trikes to purpose-built adaptive cycles, the team works with you to find the best way to get you riding, or keep you riding.

E-Bikes

Electric bikes are part of the shop's lineup — and if yours ever needs work, the service department has extensive experience with Bosch, Shimano, Bafang and Falco e-bike systems.

They don't work on exercise bikes, scooters or skateboards, electric or otherwise. For everything with pedals, email the service department at service@bikesatvienna.com or call (703) 938-8900.

Test rides

How a test ride works here

Bikes@Vienna books every test ride by email — no online booking, no walk-in slots. Their actual process:

1

Pick three or more times

Test rides run at 12:30, 2:30 and 4:30, Wednesday through Sunday. Choose THREE or more times that work for you.

2

Email the shop

Send your times to info@bikesatvienna.com. They'll do their best to accommodate you as soon as possible and confirm your slot.

3

Plan ahead

The shop is unable to make same-day appointments — so reach out a few days before you want to ride.

Request a test ride

This form opens your email app with your request addressed to info@bikesatvienna.com — exactly how the shop books test rides today. Nothing is sent until you press send in your own email.
