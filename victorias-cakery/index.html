Our Story

A Family Bakery Since 1984

Victoria’s Cakery is a second-generation family-owned and operated bakery run by Michael Hensley since 1984. Michael’s mom started the business to bring homemade cakes and other goodies to the public.

What’s more alluring than the smell of freshly baked goods? Those scents offer a moment of sweet reprieve — and this is just the right place to find that freshly baked love.
