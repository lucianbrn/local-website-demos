About All About Paws

All About Paws DMV is a pet grooming and training business in Falls Church, Virginia. Led by Carmen, the team caters to every puppy’s individual needs without using crates — and welcomes special requests from clients.

The approach is personal: care is catered to each pet’s specific needs, whether that is a full groom, a private training session, or a day built around pack walks and socialization.

For pet owners in Falls Church and the surrounding areas, it is a local, crate-free option for grooming and training in one place.
